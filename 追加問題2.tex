\begin{itembox}[l]{追加問題2}
$0 < \theta < \pi$のとき, $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos n \theta}{n}$は収束することを示せ.
\end{itembox}

$S_n:=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^n \cos k \theta \text { とおくと, } $
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{\cos k \theta}{k}=\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{k} S_k-\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{k} S_{k-1} 
=\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \frac{1}{k} S_k+\frac{1}{n} S_n-\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \frac{1}{k+1} S_k 
=\frac{1}{n} S_n-\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\left(\frac{1}{k+1}-\frac{1}{k}\right) S_k 
\end{aligned}
$$

ここで, $S_n$の値を求めるために$1+z+z^2+\cdots \cdot+z^n = \displaystyle\frac{1-z^{n+1}}{1-z}(z \neq 1) \quad に z=\cos \theta+i \sin \theta$を代入すると,

$$
(左辺)=1+(\cos \theta+i \sin \theta)+(\cos 2 \theta+i \sin 2 \theta)+\cdots \cdot+(\cos n \theta+i \sin n \theta) \\
=\sum_{k=0}^n \cos k \theta+i \sum_{k=0}^n \sin k \theta
$$

(右辺)=
$\displaystyle\frac{1-\{\cos (n+1) \theta+i \sin (n+1) \theta\}}{1-(\cos \theta+i \sin \theta)}
=\frac{2 \sin ^2 \frac{n+1}{2} \theta-2 i \sin \frac{n+1}{2} \theta \cos \frac{n+1}{2} \theta}{2 \sin ^2 \frac{\theta}{2}-2 i \sin \frac{\theta}{2} \cos \frac{\theta}{2}}$

$$\hspace*{9.5zw} = \frac{\sin \frac{n+1}{2} \theta\left(\cos \frac{n+1}{2} \theta+i \sin \frac{n+1}{2} \theta\right)}{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}\left(\cos \frac{\theta}{2}+i \sin \frac{\theta}{2}\right)}
$$

$$\hspace*{7.8zw} = \frac{\sin \frac{n+1}{2} \theta}{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}}
\left(\cos \frac{n}{2} \theta+i \sin \frac{n}{2} \theta\right)
$$
なので,実部と虚部を比較すると
$S_n = \displaystyle\frac{\sin \frac{n+1}{2} \theta \cos \frac{n}{2} \theta}{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}} - 1$ となり, $|S_n| \le \displaystyle\frac{1}{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}} + 1$.

よって, 
$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{\cos k \theta}{k} 
=\displaystyle\frac{1}{n} S_n-\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\left(\frac{1}{k+1}-\frac{1}{k}\right) S_k$
において, 

第一項は$\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty}\displaystyle\frac{1}{n} S_n = 0$なので収束し,  

第二項は$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \ \biggl|\left(\frac{1}{k+1}-\frac{1}{k}\right) S_k \biggr| \le \left(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}} + 1 \right) \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \ \biggl| \frac{1}{k+1}-\frac{1}{k} \biggr| = 
\left(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}} + 1 \right) \left(\frac{1}{n}-1\right)$

$\hspace*{30zw} \longrightarrow -\left(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}} + 1 \right)
 \ \ (n \to \infty)$ 

なので絶対収束する.
